Welcome to Lembeh

A Different Kind of Underwater Adventure.

Located in the beautiful Lembeh area of North Sulawesi, Bastianos Froggies Lembeh Dive Resort is presented here as a destination for travelers who want to explore the underwater beauty of Indonesia.

The Lembeh Strait is internationally known for its remarkable muck diving environment and unusual marine life. It offers underwater photographers and divers a unique experience.
